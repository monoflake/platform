## Question

Left open by RFC v7v3w.

Open, for the discussion that follows:

- How a host is bound: what credential its driver gives a binding, and how an admin enters it. [C3]

Narrowed by the author on 2026-10-24, RFC b75yr: the credential and how it is entered are answered
by RFCs 3d3nt and rxmd9, a binding's token made by its control and taken by its host once. One
question is left open: who may open a binding, the operator alone as now, or also an organization's
admin.
